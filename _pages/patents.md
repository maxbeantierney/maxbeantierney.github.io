## Pending Patents

**Electroosmotic Flow Cell Device**  
Co-Inventor | Patent Pending  
Tripathi Laboratory, Brown University

I built a proof-of-concept flow cell using two glass coverslips and a custom 0.1 mm PTFE gasket, forming a 0.1 mm channel with integrated electrode access points. I validated it with a dyed gel-buffer solution under an applied electric field, confirming electroosmotic flow through the channel. The lab used those results to develop the device further into its current patent-pending form.
