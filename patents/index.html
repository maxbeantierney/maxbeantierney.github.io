Electroosmotic Flow Cell Device
Co-Inventor · Tripathi Laboratory, Brown University

My contribution

I built a proof-of-concept flow cell from two glass coverslips and a custom 0.1 mm PTFE gasket, forming a 0.1 mm channel with integrated electrode access points.

I validated it with a dyed gel-buffer solution under an applied electric field, confirming electroosmotic flow through the channel. I presented the results to faculty and PhD researchers, and the lab used them to develop the device into its current patent-pending form.
