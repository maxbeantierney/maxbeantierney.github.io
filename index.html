Flagship project · Tripathi Lab × Revvity

Vivvity: automated microfluidic processing instrument

I led the mechanical and electromechanical integration of the gantry, electromagnetic clamp, pressure-control hardware, firmware, and operator interface.

Vivvity automates chip clamping and pressurization for DNA/RNA analysis on microfluidic chips. It is being developed with Revvity as the successor to the LabChip GxII Touch. In August 2026 the gantry, clamp, and pressure system ran a complete cycle, from homing to park.

  • Dual lead-screw gantry
  • Electromagnetic clamp
  • Closed-loop pressure
  • Marlin / C++ firmware
  • Python operator GUI
